Value Proposition Canvas

Customer segment: Regional managers of the 2nd and 5th Military Regions (primary users)

VALUE MAP
CUSTOMER PROFILE

Products & Services

  • Analytical outputs (infographic sets A and B, reproducible notebooks and documented tables) that complement PI-SSEx and can later feed a dashboard
  • Ingestion and curation pipeline (RAW to CURATED), open-source code and open formats
  • Versioned inclusion rule for health spending, with its coverage reported (indicator C9)
  • Reference layer: OM dimension and RM to OM, brigade to OM and UGE to OM bridges
  • Current and real values (IPCA, December 2025 reais; 2026 kept nominal)
  • Acceptance and reconciliation tests

Gain Creators

  • One place to answer a question by RM, UGE or period, without manual consolidation
  • Series comparable over time and across units
  • End-to-end traceability to the source file through the hash of the original zip
  • Objective grounds for planning and accountability

Pain Relievers

  • Central: each figure carries its inclusion rule and coverage; variation and concentration indicators (C1 to C10) lead to the records behind each amount
  • 1Unified pipeline and coverage manifest by UGE, file family and month
  • 2Historical to current reconciliation, with a rule against double counting
  • 3Real-value columns (IPCA deflation, 2014 to 2025; 2026 kept nominal)
  • 4UGE to OM bridges with validity periods; spending stays with the executing UGE until the Army completes the bridge
  • 5Current-year payments kept apart from carried-over payables
  • 6Invoice and process-number signals extracted from text, kept as candidate signals, not dimensions
  • 7Acceptance tests and RAW to governed reconciliation

Gains

  • A reconciled reference for the figures in scope: each figure shows its source and inclusion rule, which shortens disputes over the right figure; complements PI-SSEx
  • Analyses by RM, UGE, budget plan and expense element in real values, and by OM when the UGE to OM bridge is complete
  • Less time consolidating spreadsheets
  • A view of the cycle from commitment to liquidation, payment and payee
  • Figures that hold up in an audit

Pains

  • Central: the perimeter of health spending is not settled, so material variations and concentrations cannot be spotted with confidence
  • 1Data fragmented across many sources and file families
  • 2Unstable institutional universe: closures, mergers and changes of region
  • 3Nominal values distort the reading of trends
  • 4Ambiguous financial attribution between UGs and OMs
  • 5Carried-over payables mixed with current-year execution
  • 6Relevant information locked in free text
  • 7Low confidence in the figures and weak traceability

Customer Jobs

  • Central: identify the public spending verifiably related to health and spot material variations and concentrations by period, unit, procurement object and payee
  • Follow budget execution (committed, liquidated, paid, carried-over payables) by unit, region and year
  • Compare units and regions on equivalent bases
  • Justify and plan budget requests with a reliable history
  • Trace each commitment to its liquidation, payment and final payee
  • Answer oversight and audit requests
